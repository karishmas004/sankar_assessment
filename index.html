How Daybook works

Daybook asks your team a few questions each day and summarizes the answers.

  1. Invite your team

    Connect Slack or email and add teammates in under a minute.

  2. Everyone posts a quick update

    Teammates reply to a daily prompt whenever suits their schedule.

  3. Read one clean digest

    Leads get a single summary with blockers highlighted.
